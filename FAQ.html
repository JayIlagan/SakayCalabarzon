Quick Answers

How is my fare computed?

Each vehicle has a first block (4 km for jeepneys, 5 km for buses) at a flat price, then a charge for every extra kilometer. For a 10 km ride, a traditional jeepney works out to about ₱20. See the full table on the Routes and Fares page.

What is the difference between a traditional and a modern jeepney?

Traditional jeepneys are open-air, custom-built vehicles with parallel side-facing benches and a rear entrance, offering the lowest base fare. Modern jeepneys function like enclosed, air-conditioned minibuses with forward-facing seats, higher ceilings, side doors, CCTV/GPS, and cashless payment options, charging a slightly higher base fare.

What is UV Express?

UV Express (Utility Vehicle Express) is an air-conditioned van service that operates on fixed point-to-point or limited-stop routes, often connecting residential suburbs and provincial terminals directly to major transport hubs or central business districts. Fares are typically flat-rate or zone-based, and vans generally depart once full.

What is a P2P bus?

A Point-to-Point (P2P) bus is a premium express bus service that travels directly from a designated origin terminal to a specific drop-off point with no intermediate stops. Operating on fixed schedules (such as routes from Calamba or Nuvali to Makati), P2P buses offer faster travel times and higher comfort at a higher fare than regular city or provincial buses.

Who gets the 20% discount?

Under Philippine law (including Republic Act No. 11314 and related Magna Cartas), students, senior citizens, and persons with disabilities (PWDs) are entitled to a 20% fare discount on public land transportation—including jeepneys, buses, UV Express, and tricycles—upon presenting a valid school ID/enrollment proof or official Senior Citizen/PWD ID.

Is there a train from Calamba to Manila?

No. The Philippine National Railways (PNR) commuter line between Alabang and Calamba suspended operations in July 2023 to make way for the construction of the elevated North–South Commuter Railway (NSCR). Commuters traveling between Calamba and Metro Manila must currently take buses, P2P buses, or UV Express vans via SLEX.

How much is a tricycle?

There is no single nationwide rate; tricycle fares are regulated locally by each city or municipality's Local Government Unit (LGU) and local TODA (Tricycle Operators and Drivers' Association). Fares are priced either per passenger for regular shared trips along a set route, or per trip ("special ride") if you hire the tricycle privately to go directly to a specific destination.

How accurate are the travel times?

Estimated travel times on transit guides are approximations based on normal traffic flow and distance. Actual commute times in CALABARZON and Metro Manila can vary significantly depending on rush hour congestion, road construction, weather, and how long a vehicle waits at a terminal to fill seats.

Where does your information come from?

Route, vehicle, and fare data on local transit guides are typically compiled from official Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board (LTFRB) fare matrices, Department of Transportation (DOTr) advisories, LGU ordinances, and crowdsourced commuter updates—which is why the footer advises always confirming exact fares with the driver or LTFRB.
